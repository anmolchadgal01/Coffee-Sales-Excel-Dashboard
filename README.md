1. Introduction
“This is an interactive Excel dashboard I designed to visualize key metrics from coffee sales data. It allows users to filter and analyze sales by size, roast type, and customer loyalty status.”

⸻

2. Filters (Top Section)
“At the top, I’ve added slicers that let users filter data based on three categories:”
	•	Size – Coffee packaging sizes like 0.2 kg, 0.5 kg, etc.
	•	Roast Type – Options include Dark, Light, and Medium roasts.
	•	Loyalty Card – Whether a customer has a loyalty card (Yes/No).

“These filters allow dynamic interaction with the charts below, updating visuals instantly based on selection.”

⸻

3. Sales by Country (Top Right)
“This horizontal bar chart shows total sales by country. The United States has the highest sales, followed by Ireland and the United Kingdom. This helps identify top-performing regions.”

⸻

4. Top 5 Customers (Bottom Right)
“This section highlights our top customers based on sales amount. Each bar shows both quantity and total value. For example, ‘Waylin Hollingdale’ and ‘Sinclare Edsel’ are top contributors.”

⸻

5. Total Sales Over Time (Center Pie Chart)
“This pie chart shows monthly sales from January 2019 to November 2020. It helps identify seasonality or high-performing months in terms of revenue.”

⸻

6. Dashboard Design
“I used a consistent color palette and layout to ensure readability. Slicers, pivot charts, and formatting help in clear data presentation. This dashboard gives a quick overview of sales trends, customer performance, and geographic distribution.”
